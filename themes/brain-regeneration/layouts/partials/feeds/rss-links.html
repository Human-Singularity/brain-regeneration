Pages without it get an empty list, so they render exactly as before. Feeds live at {apiBase}/feed/sites/{apiSitemapSiteId}/… — built from params.apiBase (HUGO_PARAMS_APIBASE-aware), never a hard-coded host. Patent feed: links only — never abstracts or claims (EPO licence forbids syndicating them). Param: the page (.) Returns: slice of dicts {key, label, title, desc, href} */ -}} {{- $page := . -}} {{- $items := slice -}} {{- with $page.Params.feed_slug -}} {{- $slug := . -}} {{- $name := $page.Title -}} {{- $base -}} {{- if $isCondition -}} {{- $items = $items | append (dict "key" "recruiting" "label" "Recruiting trials" "title" (printf "Recruiting %s clinical trials" $name) "desc" "Trials you could join" "href" (printf "%strials/subject/%s/recruiting/" $base $slug)) -}} {{- end -}} {{- $items = $items | append (dict "key" "research" "label" "All research" "title" (printf "All %s research" $name) "desc" "Every new paper we track on this subject" "href" (printf "%sarticles/subject/%s/" $base $slug)) -}} {{- if $isCondition -}} {{- $items = $items | append (dict "key" "trials" "label" "All new
