How curation works

  1. 1

    Find

    Each lab writes the PubMed queries to find new papers in their research area. Optionally, curators can select relevant papers manually so that we can train AI models that score their relevance.

  2. 2

    Organise

    Curators can define the categories papers are sorted into. These can be mechanisms, candidate therapies, or other topics.

  3. 3

    Disseminate

    For each area there is a free weekly digest to keep researchers and patients informed.
