The [Observatório de Doenças Neurológicas](https://observatorio.abneuro.org.br/editais?utm_source=brain-regeneration&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=supporters) is run by the Academia Brasileira de Neurologia (ABN) through its REDONE.br initiative — a research network for neurological diseases in Brazil.

The Observatory maintains a public RSS feed of funding calls: grants, fellowships, and awards for neurology and neuroscience research, collected daily from CNPq, FAPESP and the state FAPs, FINEP, CAPES, NIH, Grants.gov, the European Union, AAN, Instituto Serrapilheira, and other international foundations.

Brain Regeneration's [Funding calls & opportunities](/#funding-opportunities) section on the homepage surfaces the latest neurology-relevant entries from this feed, crediting and linking back to the Observatory for the full listing.
