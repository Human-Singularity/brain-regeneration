Trials Analytics lo-fi mockup

Two companion views for brain-regeneration.com, wired live to the GregoryAI /trials/stats/ API. View 1: which conditions lag by phase, scoped to a place. View 2: who sponsors what, where, at which phase and status. Grayscale on purpose.

Phase pipeline by condition

Absolute trial counts at each phase, conditions side by side. Scope by continent or country, slide the year, and watch which condition falls behind — the amber outline marks the lowest count in each phase column.

Design note. Every cell is an exact, filtered call to /trials/stats/ — subject + place + a cumulative date_registration_before cutoff — read from by_phase and collapsed into the phase buckets shown here. No client-side approximation. Only Multiple Sclerosis, Parkinson's and Alzheimer's have trial data in this org today.

Who sponsors what, and where

Choose the condition(s) first; the matrix follows. Then narrow by place (continent or several countries), by what kind of intervention they fund, and by trial phase and status. Click a sponsor for its full profile.

Design note. Condition is the primary control. Sponsor counts and type come straight from the by_sponsor / by_sponsor_type facets for the current filters — no client-side inference. Intervention modality is the API's own category_modality field. The matrix shows counts and type only; phase mix, status split, geography and modalities load in the detail panel when you click a sponsor.
