Clinical Trials Around the World lo-fi mockup

Three layout directions for a map-driven trials explorer on brain-regeneration.com, seeded with a real sample from the GregoryAI /trials/ API. Grayscale on purpose — this is about structure and flow, not final visual design.

A · Explorer 1

The balanced workhorse. Filters on the left, world map in the middle, matching trials on the right. Click a country bubble to focus the list. Serves the doctor scanning the landscape and the patient narrowing to something joinable.

Design note. One screen, two jobs. Doctors read the map first (where is the research?); patients lean on the filters + list (what can I join?). Everything cross-filters. Wired live: each filter maps to a /trials/ query param (recruitment_status_normalized, phase_normalized, subject_id, country, age_eligible); status and phase both accept multiple picks, sent as one comma-separated OR query; the map is a proportional-symbol layer over the live per-country counts from /trials/stats/.

B · Atlas 3

Map-forward, for scanning the global picture at a glance. Big headline stats, a dark full-width map that makes clusters pop, a time scrubber to watch activity build, and a slide-up drawer with a country's trials when you click it.

Design note. Optimised for the doctor / researcher "landscape" job and for embedding as a hero on a condition page. The scrubber is bound to date_registration for an animated build-up; a production pass would swap the proportional bubbles for choropleth country shading (needs country polygons).

C · Find a trial near me 4

The patient path, shown in a phone frame because most patients arrive on mobile. Plain-language question up top, a map zoomed to their country, then recruiting trials ranked with human-readable eligibility and a clear next step.

Design note. No jargon, no dead ends. Eligibility is translated from inclusion_agemin/agemax/gender into a sentence; "Recruiting now" is the only status that leads. Age is optional and, when given, filters server-side via age_eligible — leave it blank to see trials of every age range. The same page works as a full-width web layout — the phone frame just signals the primary context. CTA opens the trial's contact / registry link.
